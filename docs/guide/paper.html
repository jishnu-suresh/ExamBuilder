🎨Make the paper yours

Look, printing, rubric, large print, versions A–D

🎨 Look: style, colours, logo

1

Exam details → 🎨 Look

Open 1 · Exam details and the 🎨 Look tab (all options):

  • Style and Colours of the cover and headings.
  • Language on the paper (English or French), question font size and line spacing.
  • School name at the bottom of every page, and the logo: change it, use the Hive Academy logo, or none.

🖨 Printing: paper size, answer sheet

2

Exam details → 🖨 Printing

The 🖨 Printing tab (all options) sets:

  • Paper size: A4 or US Letter.
  • Where students write their answers: on the paper, or on a separate answer sheet (a compact question paper, no answer lines).
  • Marks beside questions, the name field on every page, numbering again in each part, each part on a new page, the "End of exam" marker, and points per part on the cover.

Rubric page

3

A scoring page for the teacher

Switch on Rubric page (under Exam details) for a scoring page after the cover. Start from a template, or build your own groups of criteria with their points, for example an essay: ideas, organisation, language, spelling.

Choose what it lists: the parts with their points, a TOTAL row, a total grade (%) row, descriptions and a comments box.

Large print and dyslexia-friendly

4

⬇ Download → 🌻 Accommodations

Large print: 16 pt and double spacing, in one click.

Dyslexia-friendly: choose an easy-read font (Atkinson Hyperlegible or OpenDyslexic), 1.5 spacing and cream paper, and tick to get its answer key too.

Same questions, same numbers, so you can mark them with the usual key.

Versions A, B, C…

5

⬇ Download → Versions

Make 2, 3 or 4 versions so students sitting side by side get different papers (all options). Each version can shuffle:

  • the order of the questions inside each part,
  • the multiple-choice options ("All / None of the above" stay last).

Tick to download each version's answer key as well. Versions can also be large print or dyslexia-friendly.

Printing tips

  • Print at 100% ("Actual size"), not "Fit to page": otherwise the answer lines and margins shrink.
  • Print one copy first and check it before printing the whole class set.
  • For double-sided printing, choose "Flip on long edge".
