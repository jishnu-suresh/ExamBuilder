🖼Tables, graphs & pictures

Insert anything a question needs

Click where you want it in the question, then ＋ Insert ▾. It goes in at the cursor.

Tables

1

Rows × columns

Insert → Table asks for the size, such as 3x3 (a Punnett square is 3x3). Type in the cells, between the | bars. The first row is the header.

Empty cells stay blank for students to fill in.

Graphs

2

Functions or blank axes

Insert → Graph: type one or more functions, such as x^2 - 4, set the range of x and y, the grid and the labels. Leave the functions empty for blank axes students draw on.

To change it later, click 📈 Edit under the question.

Molecules

3

Draw it from its formula

Insert → Molecule: pick a common one, or type its SMILES code, such as CCO for ethanol. BEE draws the structure.

Pictures

4

Size and position

Insert → Picture and choose a photo or diagram. Under the question, set its width (5% to 100%) and position: left, centre, right, or text beside it on the left or right.

You can also drag a picture onto the question, or paste one with Ctrl/⌘+V.

5

Two pictures side by side

Add two pictures to the question, then click ⇆ Side by side on the first one. They share the line. Click it again to put them back one under the other.

A picture from a PDF

6

Cut out just the diagram

Insert → Picture from a PDF: choose the PDF, go to the page, and drag a box round the diagram. Drag its edges to adjust, then Use this part.

Handy for a diagram from a past paper or a worksheet.

Diagrams can have labels: 🏷 Label diagram on a picture puts A, B, C… or blank boxes on it for students to fill in.
