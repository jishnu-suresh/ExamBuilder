🐝Welcome

What BEE is, and a map of the screen

BEE (Build • Edit • Evaluate) turns your questions into a clean exam paper with its answer key. Students can also take the exam on a laptop, offline, with read aloud and a calculator.

It runs in your web browser. Your exams stay on your own computer: nothing is sent anywhere.

The BEE screen

Three numbered panels, from left to right: what the exam is, its questions, and the paper as it will print. Click a red number to find it in the list.

123456789
  1. 1 · Exam details: title, subject, time, instructions. Folds to one line once filled in.
  2. 2 · Questions: every question, part and text of the paper, in order.
  3. 3 · Preview: the real PDF, updated as you type. Tick Key for the answer key.
  4. Save light: green = saved in your file; orange = only in this browser.
  5. 🍯🐝 / 🌱: all options, or Basic mode with only the everyday buttons.
  6. ↩️ ↪️: undo and redo.
  7. 🕰️ History: earlier versions BEE kept while you worked.
  8. 📁 File: new, open, save as.
  9. ⬇ Download: the paper, the key, large print, versions, the computer version.
