BEE — Quick-start guide for teachers

Nov 21, 2026 · Jishnu

BEE turns your questions into a clean, printable exam paper (PDF) with an answer key. It runs in your web browser, and your exams stay on your own computer.

Open BEE

Open jishnu-suresh.github.io/ExamBuilder in Chrome or Edge. They work best, because BEE can keep saving to your exam file in these browsers. Safari and Firefox work too, but you save by hand there.

  • Install it as an app (recommended): in Chrome or Edge, click ⤓ Install app in the top bar. BEE then gets its own icon and window, and works without internet.
  • On a Mac with Safari: File → Add to Dock gives you an icon too.
  • Updates arrive by themselves. When a new version is ready, a bar says so: click Update now.

Your first exam in 5 steps

A short paper takes about 10 minutes. To look around first, click 📝 Simple quiz or ✨ Every feature on the start screen.

  1. Start a new exam. On the start screen, click ＋ New exam and choose where to save it (for example your Documents folder). This file, ending in .exam.json, is your exam: BEE saves to it every minute.
  2. Fill in the exam details: title, subject, duration, class, teacher and the instructions for students. Once filled in, this section folds to one line; click it to open it again.
  3. Add your questions, in any of these ways:
    • 🤖 Paste from AI: paste what ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini wrote. Click 💬 AI prompts for a ready-made prompt that makes the AI write questions BEE understands.
    • 📷 Add photos of a worksheet, as pictures or as text.
    • Type them: use ＋ Question, ＋ MCQ and ＋ Part at the bottom of the list.
  4. Check the preview on the right: it is the real PDF and updates as you type. Click a question in it to jump to that question. Tick Key to see the answer key.
  5. Download: click ⬇ Download ▾ at the top right, then Full exam for the students' paper and Answer key for yours.

Saving your work

Your exam is safe once it is in a project file (.exam.json). The light next to the BEE name, at the top left, tells you where your work is.

Light What it means What to do
🟢 Green Saved in your project file Nothing
🟠 Orange: “Only in this browser” Not in a file yet Click it, or 📁 File → 💾 Save as…, to make a project file
🔴 Red, with a red bar Not saved Click 💾 Save to a file in the bar
  • Open a saved exam: 📁 File → 📂 Open, or double-click the .exam.json file if BEE is installed as an app.
  • Went wrong? Go back: ↩️ undoes the last change. 🕰️ shows earlier versions BEE kept while you worked; Restore brings one back.
  • Shared or classroom computer: when you finish, click 🧹 Remove my exams from this browser. It is on the start screen and in the 🕰️ window. Otherwise the next person to open BEE on that computer can see your exam and its answer key. Your saved files are not touched.

Exams on computers (💻)

Students can also answer on a laptop, offline. You make the exam in BEE as usual; Chrome or Edge on the student laptops works best.

  1. Make the file: ⬇ Download → 👩‍🏫 Teacher → 💻 Computer version (switch off 🌱 Basic mode if you don't see it). Choose the time and options, then download. The first time on a computer, BEE asks for the school key file and its password: ask Jishnu.
  2. Note the two codes on the 🖨 invigilator sheet: the start code (give it to students when the exam starts) and the teacher code (keep it to yourself).
  3. Put the file on each laptop, for example with a USB stick. Students double-click it, type their name and the start code, and the test opens full screen.
  4. During the test: if a student leaves the test window, it beeps and locks until you type the teacher code. The teacher menu lets you add time or end the test for one student.
  5. When time is up, the screen asks the student to raise their hand. Type the teacher code, then add time or Finish: the answers are saved as one .bee-answers file.
  6. Mark: collect the answer files and open them with ⬇ Download → 👩‍🏫 Teacher → 📥 Student answers. Each becomes a PDF of the paper with the student's answers in blue.

🔊 Read aloud in your own voice. In the 💻 Computer version window, press 🎙️ Record… and read each question once (● Record, then ■ Stop). Students then hear you instead of the computer voice, on every laptop and iPad, even offline. Don't say the question number, and read multiple-choice options too. If you change a question afterwards, BEE marks its recording ⚠ text changed: record it again, or press ✓ Still right. Anything you don't record is read by the computer voice (most natural in Microsoft Edge with internet).

🧮 Calculator. In the 💻 Computer version window, choose a calculator for this exam (it starts at None):

  • BEEcalc Basic or Scientific: work offline and start in degrees. Scientific has fractions, powers, roots, trigonometry, and mean and standard deviation.
  • BEEcalc Graphing: works offline. It draws functions, circles and other curves (x² + y² = 25), shaded regions, polar graphs and sliders. It also has tables of values, data plots with regression, box plots, histograms and statistics.
  • Desmos Graphing: needs the internet in the exam room.

During the exam, the 👩‍🏫 teacher menu (with the teacher code) can switch the calculator on, change it or take it away for one student. On a phone-sized screen, graphing is replaced by Scientific; on an iPad it works fully.

Printing

Print at 100% (“Actual size”), not “Fit to page”: otherwise the answer lines and margins shrink.

  • Print one copy first and check it before printing the whole class set.
  • For double-sided printing, choose “Flip on long edge”.
  • BEE makes A4 papers. For US Letter, change it in 🖨 Printing (in Exam details).

If something goes wrong

Problem Try this
The preview doesn't update Click ↻ at the top of the preview
A question looks wrong in the PDF Check the maths between $ … $; open ⓘ next to the Questions heading for examples
I lost my changes Click 🕰️ and Restore an earlier version
A yellow bar says BEE is open twice Save your work in one tab or window, then close the other
BEE looks old or odd Click Update now if the bar shows, or reload the page

Report a problem or suggest an idea: click 💬 in the bottom-right corner of BEE, or open the BEE feedback form (sign in with your school Microsoft account). Say what you clicked, what you expected, and add a screenshot if you can.
