Paste questions from your AI (or any text)

1. Copy our prompt (or the IXL prompt for an IXL worksheet) and give it to your AI with your photo, PDF or idea. 2. Copy the AI's whole answer and paste it below.

Numbered questions become separate questions. Lines like Part I: Grammar or # Reading start a new part. Marks in square brackets at the end of a question, e.g. [5], are picked up automatically.

How to write questions (.txt / .md)

Any plain-text file works. These patterns are recognised:

# Part I: Reading            ← a line starting with # or “Part …” / “Section …” starts a part
Read the passage carefully.  ← text before the first question becomes an instruction/passage
1. Who is the narrator? [3]  ← numbered lines (1.  1)  Q1.  Question 1:) start a question
2. Choose the best answer. [2 marks]
a) Option one                ← following lines belong to the same question
b) Option two
3. Explain your reasoning. (5 marks) {lines: 6}

Part II: Writing
4. Write a 200-word essay about your summer. [20] {lines: 15}
  • Marks: [5], [5 marks] or (5 marks) at the end of the first or last line.
  • Answer lines: add {lines: 6} to print 6 ruled lines under a question.
  • If there are no numbers at all, each paragraph (separated by a blank line) becomes a question.
  • Photos become their own question — add text or marks to it afterwards.

Maths, science & formatting

5. Solve $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$. [3]           ← $…$ = maths inside a sentence (LaTeX)
6. Evaluate: [4]
$$\int_0^1 \frac{x^2}{\sqrt{1+x^3}}\,dx$$     ← $$…$$ = equation on its own line, centred
7. Balance $\ce{Fe + O2 -> Fe2O3}$ [2]         ← \ce{…} = chemistry (formulas, arrows, ions)
8. A car accelerates at $3\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ for $5\,\mathrm{s}$. **Find** its *final* speed.
| Time (s) | 0 | 1 | 2 |                     ← a Markdown table
|---|---|---|---|
| Distance (m) | 0 | 4 | 16 |
  • {numberline: -10..10} and {axes: -6..6} after the marks give a number line or a coordinate grid to answer on; {mol: CCO} on its own line draws a molecule.
  • {center} or {right} at the start of a line aligns it, {big} / {bigger} / {huge} / {small} changes its size; 🖼 Picture here puts a picture exactly where the cursor is.
  • **bold**, *italic*, ++underline++, ~~strikethrough~~, and lines starting with - become bullet points.
  • Don't know LaTeX? Use ∑ Equation — click buttons to build the formula; it writes the LaTeX for you.
  • A price like “$5 and $10” stays as normal text. Write \$ if a dollar sign is ever mistaken for maths.
  • Sub-questions: lines like (a) Name the gas. [1] {lines: 2} inside a question become parts (a), (b)… with their own marks.
  • Answer key: {correct} after the right MCQ option, and a line Model answer: … under a question or sub-question. Both only appear on the answer key.
  • Multiple choice: add {mcq} (one answer, ○ bubbles) or {mcq: multi} (several answers, ☐ boxes); {cols: 2} puts options in 2 columns. Or choose Type on the question card.
  • Biology: *Escherichia coli* → italics; ▦ Table on a card inserts a table (Punnett squares, results tables); 🏷 Label diagram on an image adds label lines for students.
  • Chemistry examples: \ce{H2SO4}, \ce{Na+ + Cl- -> NaCl}, \ce{N2 + 3H2 <=> 2NH3}, \ce{CaCO3 v}.

📄 BEE can't open PDF files

A PDF is made for printing, so BEE can't take the questions out of it. Your AI tool can do that in a few seconds, using BEE's ready-made prompt:

  1. Copy BEE's prompt.
  2. Open your AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot…), attach your PDF, paste the prompt and send.
  3. Copy the AI's answer and paste it into BEE.

Simple diagrams are drawn by the AI; other pictures come back as red “picture missing” boxes. BEE keeps this PDF open: fill those boxes with 📄 Cut from PDF.

Equation editor

Type normally: / makes a fraction, ^ a power, _ a subscript, sqrt a root, pi → π, <= → ≤. Arrow keys move between boxes.
Write formulas as you would by hand — numbers become subscripts automatically. Arrows: -> <=> <-. Charges: Na+, SO4^2-. States: (aq) (s). Gas ↑ ^, precipitate ↓ v.

🐝 The school key

Every computer version is sealed with the school key. Any teacher whose BEE has the school key, and who knows its password, can open any student's answers, for every exam.

🔐 Teacher password

Dyslexia-friendly exam

The same paper, easier to read: a clear font, at least 12 pt, 1.5 line spacing, text not stretched to both edges, and more space between questions.

Print versions A, B, C…

Each version has the same questions in a different order, so students sitting side by side get different papers. Your exam itself doesn't change — the shuffling only happens in the downloaded PDFs, and it is the same every time (Version B is always the same Version B).

Questions never move past a part heading or a text/passage, so they stay with the reading, table or picture they belong to. Your browser may ask once to allow several downloads.
