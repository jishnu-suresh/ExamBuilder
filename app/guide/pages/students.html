Answering

Each question has an answer box. ＋ Text, ＋ Maths and ＋ Drawing add more boxes to the same answer.

Multiple-choice questions have options to click.

＋ Maths opens a maths box with an on-screen maths keyboard: fractions, powers, roots, π, units. √x Maths keyboard at the top switches it on or off.

The 🚩 next to a question marks it to come back to. The question list on the left shows answered and 🚩 questions.

With the drawing pad on, students can sketch, draw graphs and label diagrams: pen, straight line, labels, eraser, colours and thickness, then ✓ Done. The pad can be blank, a grid, axes, a number line or the question's picture.

Reading help

Aa changes text size, line spacing, the font (Easy-read or OpenDyslexic), colours (white, cream, dark), a coloured overlay, letter spacing and a big pointer.

📏 Ruler shows a reading band that follows the line being read.

Click 🖍 Highlight, then select words in a question to highlight them. Click a highlight to remove it.
Text size, fonts and colours are always there. When you make the file, you choose whether students may also use the coloured overlay, the ruler, wider spacing, the highlighter and rest breaks. é Accents adds French accents for typing.
