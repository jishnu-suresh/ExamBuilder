Maths and equations

Put maths between dollar signs: $x^2 + 1$, $\frac{3}{4}$, $\sqrt{49}$. The line under the question shows how it will look.

Prefer clicking? ＋ Insert ▾ → Equation opens a visual editor: / makes a fraction, ^ a power, arrow keys move between boxes. It has palettes for algebra, geometry, sets, Greek letters, biology and physics units, plus LaTeX and Chemistry tabs.

Choose Inside the sentence or On its own line, then Insert equation.
Not sure how to write something? Click ⓘ next to the Questions heading for examples.

Bold, underline, colour, size

Select a word and click B, I, U or a colour in the bar above the question. Select nothing to format the whole question.

A− and A+ make lines smaller or bigger, and the alignment buttons move them left, centre or right.

Questions with (a), (b), (c)

Type the shared start of the question, then click ＋ (a). Each sub-question has its own marks, answer space and model answer.

The question's marks become the sum of its sub-questions.

Answer space

Under each question, Answer sets where students write: Lines, Blank space, Grid, Box, a Number line or a Coordinate grid (x, y). The box next to it sets how many lines, or how tall.

A number line takes its range, such as -10..10; a coordinate grid takes -6..6, -6..6.
Want all answers on a separate sheet instead? That is one switch for the whole exam: see Printing.

Model answers for the key

Click ✎ Answer on a question and type the model answer or the marking points. It shows in red on the answer key only, never on the students' paper.

For multiple choice, the ✓ you set is the key.

⋯ More: half width, no number…

⋯ More ▾ on a question has the less-used switches (all options):

  • Half width: two short questions side by side.
  • No number: print it without a question number.
  • Hide marks: don't print [marks] (they still count).
  • Start on a new page, and Line numbers for reading passages.

Whatever is on shows as a small chip under the question. Click its ✕ to switch it off.

Passages, sub-headings and page breaks

＋ Text adds instructions or a reading passage, without a number. ＋ Sub-heading adds a heading inside a part, such as A. Understanding the text. ＋ Page break starts what follows on a new page.

Arrange and tidy

⇅ Arrange shows the whole exam as small tiles, part by part. Drag a tile to move it, or type Move question 9 to position 7 and click Move.

On each question, ↑ ↓ move it one place, ⧉ copies it and ✕ deletes it (↩️ brings it back).

Click 🔎 next to the Questions heading, type the word to find and what to put instead. BEE lists every place, then Replace all. (all options)
