If something goes wrong

ProblemTry this
The preview doesn't updateClick ↻ at the top of the preview
A question looks wrong in the PDFCheck the maths between $ … $; open ⓘ next to the Questions heading for examples
I lost my changesClick 🕰️ History and Restore an earlier version
A yellow bar says BEE is open twiceSave your work in one tab or window, then close the other
BEE looks old or oddClick Update now if the bar shows, or reload the page
A saved exam won't openUse 📁 File → 📂 Open and choose the .exam.json file, in Chrome or Edge
A student's test is lockedType the teacher code on the lock screen
Read aloud is silentCheck the laptop's sound, and that read aloud was on when the file was made
Desmos doesn't loadIt needs the internet; choose BEEcalc Graphing for offline rooms
