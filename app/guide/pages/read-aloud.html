In the computer version

When read aloud is on, 🔊 Read aloud in the top bar reads from the part on the screen, and each part has its own 🔊 button.

A reading bar appears at the bottom: back ⏮, pause ⏸, stop ⏹, next ⏭, the speed 🐢–🐇, the voice, and Keep reading to carry on to the next question.

BEE reads maths, chemistry and units in words, and pauses before each multiple-choice option.

The read-aloud version

In the 💻 Computer version window, choose 🔊 Read-aloud only. Students see the paper on screen with every part read aloud, and write their answers on the printed paper.

▶ Read from the start reads the whole paper, part after part.

In your own voice

In the 💻 Computer version window, press 🎙️ Record… and read each question once: ● Record, then ■ Stop. ▶ Play to listen. Students then hear you instead of the computer voice, on every laptop, even offline.

  • Don't say the question number, because versions A–D can change it.
  • For multiple choice, read the options too.
  • Anything you don't record is read by the computer voice.
  • If you change a question after recording it, BEE marks it ⚠ text changed: record it again, or press ✓ Still right.
For the most natural computer voice, use Microsoft Edge with internet, or download a Premium voice on a Mac (System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content).
