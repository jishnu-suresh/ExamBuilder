A short paper takes about 10 minutes. These 5 steps take you from an empty exam to the PDF you print.

5 steps

In 1 · Exam details, type the title, subject, duration and your name. Each line of Instructions becomes a bullet on the cover.

Leave Class or Date blank and students fill them in on the paper. Total marks adds itself up from the questions.

Once filled in, the panel folds to one line. Click it to open it again.

Click ＋ Question at the bottom of the list and type. For maths, put it between dollar signs: $\frac{3}{4}$ becomes ¾.

Under the question, set its Marks and the Answer space: lines, a box, a grid, a number line or a coordinate grid.

Click ＋ MCQ, type the question, then one option per box. Press Enter in an option to add the next one.

Click ✓ next to the right answer. It turns green, and shows on the answer key only.

Click ＋ Part and give it a name, such as Part B: Problem solving. The questions after it belong to that part, and the cover lists each part with its marks.

The 3 · Preview on the right is the real PDF. It updates as you type. Click a question in it to jump to that question.

Tick Key to see the answer key: correct options are ticked and model answers are shown in red.

Click ⬇ Download ▾ at the top right, then Full exam for the students' paper, and Answer key for yours.

Before printing the class set, print one copy at 100% (Actual size) and check it.
