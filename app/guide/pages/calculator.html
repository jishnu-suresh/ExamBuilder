Choose a calculator

🧮 Calculator starts at None each time. Choose one for this exam:

  • BEEcalc Basic or Scientific: work offline, start in degrees.
  • BEEcalc Graphing: works offline.
  • Desmos Graphing: needs the internet in the exam room.

Students open it with 🧮 Calculator in the top bar. It floats over the paper and can be moved by its title bar.

BEEcalc Scientific

Tap the keys or type, then Enter. It has fractions (S⇔D switches between ¾ and 0.75), powers, roots, trigonometry in degrees or radians, logs, and mean and stdev of a list such as [3, 5, 8].

Earlier answers stay listed above, and Ans uses the last one.

BEEcalc Graphing

Type y = x^2 - 4, then ＋ Expression for the next one. BEE marks where curves cross each other and the axes. Drag to move; zoom with the mouse wheel, a pinch, or ＋ −; ⌂ goes back to the start. Click a curve to trace it.

Any equation in x and y: x^2 + y^2 = 25 is a circle. Inequalities such as y < 2x + 1 shade the region.

Polar graphs start with r =, such as r = 4cos(2θ), with the θ range under them.

Use a letter that has no value, such as y = m x + b, and BEE offers a slider for it. Drag the slider to see the graph change.

＋ Table plots points. If they are far from 0 (years, say), the graph moves to them by itself; 🔍 Zoom to fit brings them back at any time.

Fit ▾ finds the best line or curve: linear, quadratic, exponential and more. 📉 Residuals on the fit adds a column e1 (y − the fitted value) and plots the residuals. 📊 Statistics gives n, mean, median, quartiles, standard deviation and the correlation r.

A list such as L = [1, 2, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7], then boxplot(L) or histogram(L, 2).

Change it during the exam

On a student's screen, the 👩‍🏫 teacher menu (with the teacher code) can switch the calculator on, change it or take it away. Each change is noted in the report.
On a phone-sized screen, graphing is replaced by BEEcalc Scientific. On an iPad it works fully, with your fingers.
