From ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot…

Click 💬 AI prompts ▾ → Generic AI prompt, then Copy prompt. Paste it into your AI, with your worksheet, photo or PDF, or describe the exam you want.

The prompt makes the AI write questions in a form BEE understands: parts, marks, options, answers, even diagrams. There is also one for IXL printed worksheets.

Copy the AI's whole answer, click 🤖 Paste from AI, paste it in and click Add questions.

BEE counts the questions as you paste. The prompt asks the AI for this simple form, which you can also type yourself:

WrittenBecomes
Part I: Algebra [20]a part, with its marks
1. Solve the equation. [3]question 1, worth 3 marks
{lines: 4} after the marks4 answer lines
{mcq}, then a) … b) …a multiple-choice question; {correct} after the right option ticks it
(a) Name the gas. [1]a sub-question with its own marks
Model answer: …the answer on the key
Always read what the AI wrote before printing. It can make mistakes in answers and marks.

From photos

Click 📷 Add photos and choose one or more photos. BEE asks how to add them:

  • Pictures: each photo goes into the paper as it is.
  • Text: BEE reads the words (offline, on your computer) so you can edit them.

Tick Remember my choice to skip the question next time.
For handwriting, lots of maths or a long PDF, the AI prompt above usually reads it better than the photo reader.

From a text file

📄 Open .txt / .md reads a text or Markdown file, written the same way as the AI's answer. You can also drop the file onto the question list.
